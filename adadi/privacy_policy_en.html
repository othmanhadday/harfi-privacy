Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-08

Summary

Adadi is a learning game for children, made by HabSolutions. We do not ask for a name, email address, photo or location, and the game has no accounts or chat.

What is saved on the device

Game progress (levels, stars, scores and hearts) and settings (language, music, voice and background) are saved only on this device. We do not receive this information.

Advertising

The game shows ads provided by Google AdMob. We ask Google to treat every ad request as coming from a child, to show only ads suitable for all ages, and not to personalize ads. The game does not use the advertising ID. To deliver ads, measure them and prevent fraud, Google may process technical data such as the IP address and device information. Google's privacy policy is available at policies.google.com/privacy.

Internet and voice

The game uses the internet only to load ads and works without ads when offline. Numbers and questions read aloud use the text-to-speech service already on the device, so they follow that service's own settings.

Parents

Buttons that share the game or open the Play Store are protected by an adult question. You can delete everything saved by the game by uninstalling it or by clearing its data in the device settings.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will appear in the game with a new date.

Contact

For any question or request about privacy, write to othday.contact@gmail.com.
